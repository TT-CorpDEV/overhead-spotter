Where data comes from

Aircraft: community ADS-B receiver networks. Satellites: orbital elements from CelesTrak. Sun and Moon: computed on your phone. Live mode sends your rounded position to the flight feed to ask what is nearby. Ships need an AIS data key and show only in the sample sky for now. Your logbook is saved in this browser only.
