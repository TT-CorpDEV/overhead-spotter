02Set up on your phone

Steps for iPhone. On Android instead?Steps for Android. On an iPhone instead?Pick your phone below.

iPhoneSAFARI · 3 STEPS
  1. Open the invite link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open the app from that new icon.
  3. Go to Hangar, tap Join with a code, enter the code from the text and your name.
Why the extra step? iPhone keeps home-screen apps separate from Safari. Join from the icon, or your progress stays stuck in the browser.
SEAT 1A
AndroidCHROME · 2 STEPS
  1. Tap the invite link. It opens in Chrome and asks for your name. You're in.
  2. Optional but recommended: tap Install app (or Chrome menu, then Add to Home screen) so it opens full screen.
First launch: allow location, motion and camera. Alerts are optional and handy for rare flyovers.

06House rules

  • No cheating possible. The server counts your points from your logbook, so nobody can type in a fake score.
  • Friends see only your name, points, level, badges and best catch. Never your location, contacts or email.
  • Up to 50 pilots in the club. The captain starts it and sends the invites.
  • Eyes up, not on the road. Spot from the passenger seat, the porch or the park.

07Quick FAQ

I changed my mind about my name.

Rename yourself anytime in Hangar.

I got a new phone / cleared my browser.

Your spot lives on your phone. Rejoin with the code and you start fresh at zero, so don't delete the app.

The plane isn't lining up.

Open Settings and run the compass alignment, then wave the phone in a figure 8.

Does it cost anything?

Nope. Free, no ads, no store.
