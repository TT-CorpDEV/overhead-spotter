[ Setup ]

Set up on your phone.

Pick your phone. It's the same app either way, installed straight from the web.

  1. Open it in Safari

    Tap Get the app on this page. It has to be Safari.

  2. Add to Home Screen

    Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the new icon.

  3. Join the leaderboard

    Go to Hangar, tap Join the leaderboard and pick your name.

Why the home screen? iPhone keeps home-screen apps separate from Safari. Join from the icon, or your progress stays stuck in the browser.

  1. Open it in Chrome

    Tap Get the app on this page.

  2. Install the app

    Tap Install app, or Chrome's menu, then Add to Home screen.

  3. Join the leaderboard

    Go to Hangar, tap Join the leaderboard and pick your name.

First launch: allow location, motion and camera so the app knows where you are and where you're pointing. Alerts are optional.

[ House rules ]

Fair play, by design.

  • Scores are server-counted. Points come from your logbook, so nobody can type in a fake score.
  • Friends see only your name, points, level, badges and best catch. Never your location, contacts or email.
  • Up to 50 spotters. Anyone with the link can join.
  • Eyes up, not on the road. Spot from the passenger seat, the porch or the park.

[ FAQ ]

Quick answers.

Can I change my name?
Anytime, in Hangar.
New phone, or cleared my browser?
Your spot lives on your phone. Rejoin from Hangar and you start fresh at zero, so don't delete the app.
The plane isn't lining up.
Open Settings and run the compass alignment, then move the phone in a figure 8.
Does it cost anything?
No. Free, no ads, no app store.
